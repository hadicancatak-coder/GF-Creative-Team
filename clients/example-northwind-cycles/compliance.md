## Mandated text
**Range disclaimer**, verbatim, on any creative stating a range figure:
> "Range varies with rider weight, terrain, temperature and assist level. Tested per EN 15194."

Ink `#41535C`, opacity 1, minimum 14px, left-aligned under the claim — never in the footer band alone.

**DE only** additionally requires the pedelec class line. **Text still `TBD` with client legal — DE
creatives carrying a range figure cannot ship** (eval U23). DE creatives without a range figure are fine.

## Claims
- Range: **Drift up to 65 km, Ridge up to 48 km.** Never state a range without the disclaimer adjacent.
- Charge time: **4.5 hours to 100%** (Drift), **5 hours** (Ridge).
- Never imply the bike replaces a car for a specific journey type, or state any cost-saving figure —
  the "£1,400/year vs driving" number in the 2024 deck was never substantiated and is retired.
- No comparative claims against named competitors.
- No rider shown without a helmet in DE or NL creatives.
