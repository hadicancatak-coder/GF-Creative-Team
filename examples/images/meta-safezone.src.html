What is real here is the geometry. Meta publishes a reserve of approximately 14% top, 35% bottom and 6% each side for Stories and Reels — source and verification date in knowledge/platforms/meta.md. The elements are drawn as blocks on purpose: this diagram measures a constraint, it does not demonstrate a layout. A design analyst flags the middle frame as a BLOCKER under eval U25, and it is not visible in the design file at 100% zoom.
